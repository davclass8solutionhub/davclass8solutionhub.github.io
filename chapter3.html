D. Answer the following questions in brief.

1. Name one metal and one non-metal which normally exist in liquid state at room temperature.
Answer: Mercury metal and bromine non-metal normally exist in liquid state at room temperature.
2. State the fraction of gold present in an ornament made of 22-carat gold.
Answer: The fraction of gold present in an ornament made of 22-carat gold is 22/24, which means 22 parts of pure gold are mixed with 2 parts of copper or silver.
3. State the property of copper wires that makes them useful for electrical fittings.
Answer: The property of copper wires that makes them useful for electrical fittings is their high electrical conductivity, which allows electric current to flow through them easily.
4. Define an alloy.
Answer: An alloy is defined as a homogeneous mixture of two or more metals, or a metal and a non-metal.
5. Why are noble metals used for making jewellery?
Answer: Noble metals are used for making jewellery because they are least reactive and do not get tarnished, so they retain their metallic lustre for a very long time.

E. Answer the following questions.

1. Give reasons for the following:
(a) Aluminium is used for making cooking vessels.
(b) Sodium is stored under kerosene.
Answer:
(a) Aluminium is used for making cooking vessels because it has high thermal conductivity and it is a very light metal.
(b) Sodium is stored under kerosene because it is highly reactive and reacts vigorously with oxygen and moisture present in the air, catching fire instantly.
2. Write the balanced chemical equations for the following reactions:
(a) Sodium reacts with water.
(b) Magnesium reacts with dilute hydrochloric acid.
Answer: The balanced chemical equations for the given reactions are:
(a) 2Na(s) + 2H2O(l) → 2NaOH(aq) + H2(g)
(b) Mg(s) + 2HCl(aq) → MgCl2(aq) + H2(g)
3. What is the reactivity series of metals? How does it help us in predicting the relative reactivity of metals?
Answer: The reactivity series of metals is the series in which metals are arranged in the order of their decreasing reactivity. It helps us predict relative reactivity because the metals placed at the top are most reactive, while the metals placed at the bottom are least reactive.
4. Differentiate between metals and non-metals on the basis of their physical properties.
Answer: On the basis of their physical properties, metals and non-metals differentiate from each other as follows:
Physical Property Metals Non-metals
Physical State Metals are generally solids at room temperature, except mercury which is a liquid. Non-metals can exist in all three states: solids, liquids, or gases.
Metallic Lustre Metals have a shiny surface known as metallic lustre. Non-metals generally have a dull appearance, except iodine which has a natural lustre.
Malleability & Ductility Metals are highly malleable (can be beaten into sheets) and ductile (can be drawn into wires). Non-metals are brittle and break into pieces when hammered, meaning they are neither malleable nor ductile.
Conductivity Metals are good conductors of heat and electrical conductivity. Non-metals are poor conductors of heat and electricity, except graphite which is a good conductor.
