Section D: Answer the Following Questions in Brief

1. State any two ways which can lead to contamination of water.

Answer: Two ways that lead to contamination of water are the direct discharge of untreated domestic sewage into rivers and industrial chemical wastes entering water bodies.

2. Give the meaning of the term 'water pollutants'.

Answer: The unwanted and harmful substances like sewage, toxic chemicals, and agricultural wastes that contaminate water bodies are called water pollutants.

3. Name two of the main diseases that can be caused by drinking contaminated water.

Answer: Two main water-borne diseases are cholera and jaundice (hepatitis).

4. Name two chemicals that may help in purifying water.

Answer: Two chemicals that help in purifying water are chlorine gas (or bleaching powder) and potassium permanganate.

5. Name the kind of 'growths' in water that lead to a depletion of the oxygen dissolved in it.

Answer: The rapid growth of algae (known as algal bloom) leads to a depletion of the dissolved oxygen in water.

Section E: Answer the Following Questions

1. List the main parameters that need to be followed before water is supplied for drinking purposes.

The main parameters are that the water must be completely free from suspended particles, odorless, colorless, free from harmful microscopic pathogens, and contain essential dissolved minerals.

2. Write, in brief:
(i) one physical method that can be used for 'water purification'.

(i) One physical method is boiling, which involves heating water to its boiling point to destroy all active harmful germs and bacteria present in it.

(ii) one chemical method that can be used for 'water purification'.

(ii) One chemical method is chlorination, which involves adding chlorine tablets or bleaching powder to water to effectively kill harmful microbes.

3. State the measures that must be taken to check water pollution by sewage.

Measures are needed because untreated sewage carries dangerous pathogens, so we must set up advanced sewage treatment plants to purify wastewater before it reaches rivers, and strictly ban open defecation near water streams.

4. Suggest some simple steps that can be taken to help in conserving water.

Simple steps include fixing leaking taps immediately, using a bucket instead of a running hose pipe to wash cars, implementing rainwater harvesting systems in houses, and using a half-flush setup in toilets.
