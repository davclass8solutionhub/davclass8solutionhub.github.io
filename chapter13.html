D. Answer the Following Questions in Brief

1. Define the term 'synthetic fibres'.

Answer: Man-made fibres obtained by chemical processing of petrochemicals are called synthetic fibres.

2. Write two uses of rayon.

Answer: Two uses of rayon are making apparels like shirts and home furnishings like bedsheets.

3. Name two to three articles that can be made from nylon.

Answer: Three articles made from nylon are socks, ropes, and toothbrushes.

4. Why are clothes made from synthetic fibres uncomfortable in summer?

Answer: Clothes made from synthetic fibres are uncomfortable in summer because they absorb very little sweat and trap body heat.

5. Give two examples each of thermoplastics and thermosetting plastics.

Answer: Two examples of thermoplastics are PVC and polythene, while two examples of thermosetting plastics are bakelite and melamine.

E. Answer the Following Questions

1. How is a 'regenerated fibre' different from a 'true synthetic fibre'? Give one example of each of these two types of fibres.

Answer: A regenerated fibre is made from natural cellulose modified by chemicals (Example: Rayon), whereas a true synthetic fibre is made completely from chemicals without any natural raw material (Example: Nylon).

2. Why do clothes made of nylon last longer?

Answer: Clothes made of nylon last longer because nylon is a highly strong, elastic, and abrasion-resistant fibre that does not tear easily.

3. Write two points of differences between thermosetting plastics and thermoplastics.

Answer: Thermoplastics can be easily softened and remoulded repeatedly on heating, whereas thermosetting plastics cannot be softened or remoulded once molded into a shape.

4. State any three advantages of synthetic fibres.

Answer: Three advantages of synthetic fibres are that they are very strong, dry quickly, and are wrinkle-resistant.

5. Give your views on the following: 'Excessive use of plastics is a threat to environment.'

Answer: Excessive use of plastics is a major threat to the environment because plastics are non-biodegradable, take hundreds of years to decompose, clog drains, choke animals, and cause severe land and water pollution.

6. Why is bakelite a good choice for making electrical switches and handles of cooking utensils?

Answer: Bakelite is a good choice for making electrical switches and handles of cooking utensils because it is a poor conductor of electricity and can resist heat effectively without melting.
