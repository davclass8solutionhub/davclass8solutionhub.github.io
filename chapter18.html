Section D: Answer the Following Questions in Brief

1. What is an earthquake?

Answer: An earthquake is a sudden shaking or trembling of the earth's surface caused by a disturbance deep inside the earth's crust.

2. State any three of the 'damaging effects' of an earthquake.

Answer: Three damaging effects of an earthquake are destruction of buildings and infrastructure, loss of human lives, and triggering of landslides or tsunamis.

3. Explain the phenomenon of production of shock waves.

Shock waves are produced because when tectonic plates slide past each other or collide, they get locked due to friction, and when the accumulated stress breaks the lock, a sudden release of energy travels outwards in the form of seismic waves.

4. In the context of an earthquake, which of the two points: the focus or the epicenter, is deep under the ground?

The focus is the point which is located deep under the ground.

5. Name the scale used for measuring the strength of an earthquake. Write the likely magnitude of (a) a mild earthquake (b) a highly destructive earthquake on this scale.

The scale used is the Richter scale. (a) The magnitude of a mild earthquake is below 3.0. (b) The magnitude of a highly destructive earthquake is above 7.0.

6. An earthquake measures 3 on Richter Scale.
(a) Would it be recorded on the seismometer?

(a) Yes, it would be recorded on the seismometer.

(b) Is it likely to cause much damage?

(b) No, it is not likely to cause much damage because its magnitude is very low.

Section E: Answer the Following Questions

1. Explain, in brief, the cause of an earthquake.

An earthquake is caused because the earth's outermost layer is broken into separate tectonic plates that constantly move, and when they brush past or collide with each other, it causes tremors on the crust.

2. Where are the earthquakes more likely to occur?

Earthquakes are more likely to occur along the boundaries of tectonic plates, which are known as seismic zones or fault zones.

3. Mr. Sarthak is the head of a construction company. He got a contract to design and build a housing complex in an area which was under the 'seismic zone'. State the main precautions which Mr. Sarthak's company should take so that houses, built by it, are durable.

The company should build earthquake-resistant structures, use lightweight building materials like mud or timber instead of heavy concrete, and make sure that fire fighting systems are properly installed inside the complex.

4. State the precautions, that one should take for protection during an earthquake if one is
(a) inside the house

(a) Take shelter under a sturdy table, stay away from tall and heavy objects, and do not use the lift.

(b) outdoors

(b) Move out into an open area away from buildings, tall trees, and overhead power lines.

5. State the main precautions that can be taken to minimise the effect of an earthquake.

The effects can be minimised because we can construct buildings according to anti-seismic design rules, train people through emergency mock drills, and use timber or mud for construction in high-risk zones.
