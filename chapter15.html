D. Answer the Following Questions in Brief

1. State the meaning of the term Adolescence.

Answer: Adolescence is the period of life between childhood and adulthood when the body undergoes changes leading to reproductive maturity.

2. Define puberty. State the various factors which influence it.

Answer: Puberty is the specific time period during adolescence when an individual becomes sexually mature and capable of reproduction. It is influenced by factors like hormones, nutrition, and genetic background.

3. Why are endocrine glands known as ductless glands?

Answer: Endocrine glands are known as ductless glands because they do not have specific tubes or ducts to carry their secretions, so they release hormones directly into the bloodstream.

4. Why do tadpoles growing in iodine deficient waters fail to grow into frogs?

Answer: Tadpoles growing in iodine deficient waters fail to grow into frogs because the production of thyroxine hormone requires iodine, and without enough thyroxine, the process of metamorphosis cannot take place.

5. How does the pituitary gland control the maturation of the gonads?

Answer: The pituitary gland controls the maturation of the gonads by secreting stimulating hormones that instruct the testes in males and ovaries in females to produce their respective sex hormones.

6. Women, above the age of fifty, are (generally) unable to give birth to children. Explain the reason for the same.

Answer: Women above the age of fifty are unable to give birth because they reach menopause, a stage where the ovaries stop releasing eggs permanently and the production of female sex hormones stops.

E. Answer the Following Questions

1. Enlist the changes which occur in males and females during puberty.

Answer: During puberty, common changes include sudden increase in height, growth of body hair, development of acne, maturation of sex organs, and production of sex hormones.

2. Arun and his sister Arunima are both teenagers. Arunima observes a swelling on her neck, following which she visits a doctor. The doctor identifies it to be a deficiency disease and prescribes medicines accordingly along with suggesting dietary modifications. A few months later, Arun observes a small protrusion on his throat and visits the doctor. However, the doctor assures him that it is a normal feature in boys of his age.

(a) Identify the disease Arunima was suffering from along with its cause.

(a) Arunima was suffering from Goitre, which is caused by the deficiency of iodine in the diet leading to an underactive thyroid gland.

(b) Why do you think that no medication was prescribed for Arun?

(b) No medication was prescribed for Arun because the small protrusion on his throat was simply Adam's apple, which is a natural enlargement of the larynx that happens normally in growing teenage boys.

3. Discuss how the sex of the unborn child gets determined in human beings.

Answer: The sex of an unborn child is determined by sex chromosomes. A female egg always has an X chromosome, while male sperms can have either an X or a Y chromosome; if an X-carrying sperm fertilises the egg, the child is a girl (XX), and if a Y-carrying sperm fertilises it, the child is a boy (XY).

4. Enlist the events that regularly occur in the female reproductive system, after the onset of puberty, until menopause.

Answer: The regular event is the menstrual cycle, where an egg matures and is released by an ovary every 28-30 days, the uterine wall thickens to receive it, and if fertilisation does not occur, the lining breaks down and is shed as bleeding (menstruation).

5. Highlight the importance of exercise and good personal hygiene during adolescence.

Answer: Exercise keeps the growing body fit and active, while good personal hygiene is important because increased activity of sweat and sebaceous glands can cause body odour and skin infections if the body is not kept clean daily.
