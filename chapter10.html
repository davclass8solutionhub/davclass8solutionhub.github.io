D. Answer the Following Questions in Brief

1. Light propagates faster through a Medium A than through another Medium B.
(a) Which of the two media has a higher optical density?

Answer: Medium B has a higher optical density because light travels slower in an optically denser medium.

(b) State the condition under which no 'change in the direction of propagation' of a light ray would take place when it passes from Medium A to Medium B.

Answer: There will be no change in the direction of propagation when the ray falls normally on the interface, that is, when the angle of incidence is 0°.

2. Why does a pencil appear bent when it is partially dipped in water?

Answer: A pencil appears bent when it is partially dipped in water because the light rays coming from the submerged part pass from one medium to another, that is, from water to air, and undergo refraction. The rays bend away from the normal, due to which the submerged part appears raised and the pencil looks bent.

4. A lens is held towards the Sun and a sharp image is obtained on a black paper. The hole in the paper burns.

(a) Which lens is used?

Answer: A convex lens, also called a converging lens, is used.

(b) What is the distance between the lens and the black paper?

Answer: The distance between the lens and the black paper is equal to the focal length of the lens.

(c) What is the advantage of using black paper instead of white paper?

Answer: Black paper absorbs more heat energy than white paper, so it reaches its ignition temperature faster and burns quickly.

4. With the help of a suitable diagram, explain:

(a) Centres of curvature

The centres of curvature are the centres of the two spheres of which the surfaces of a lens form a part.

(b) Optical centre

The optical centre is a point on the principal axis of a lens through which a ray of light passes without deviation.

(c) Focus

The focus is the point on the principal axis where rays parallel to the principal axis converge, or appear to diverge, after refraction through a lens.
